How a rule moves

One motion per part. Bob can stack axes (up and down, plus a side sway). Spin and slide take an expression of t (seconds) and the sliders. lineSpan is spacing × (count − 1). Follow rides another part, about that part's pivot. A rope rebuilds its free span as a sagging curve between two ends; a coiled end stays rigid on the part it wraps. Sag is the fraction of the span length that the middle hangs.
